Dance Floor Freak News

Peter Rauhofer Will release his new album "I love New York" On Tuesday The 7th of march. This flawless beatmix 2xCD has 24 incredible underground club tracks including 20 never released on CD before. This CD showcases Peter Rauhofer's versatile style and proves again why he is one of the most influential in this industry! He was also working on remixing Paris Hilton's new track "turn it up"

Legendary German DJ Paul van Dyk was recently named #1 DJ in the world by DJ Magazine. A proven leader of his genre, van Dyk was one of the first artists to receive a Grammy nomination in the newly added category of Best Dance/Electronic album for his 2004 release Reflections. His latest album The Politics of Dancing Volume 2 was released last year and remains in the top 20 Electronic Album charts.

A feature about Pet Shop Boys can be found in the latest (April) issue of the British "GQ" magazine. Their new single will be released On May 8th and is called "I'm With stupid". The New album "Fundamental" Will be released May 22nd.

Robbie Rivera is Currently in the studio remixing the classic song: Sandy B "you make the world go round" for release soon on Champion UK.
